Reading the two measures

Physical (P)

Estimated from the history of the short rate itself. Says where rates are likely to go. κ is the speed of mean reversion, θ the level rates drift to, σ the size of the shocks.

Risk-neutral (Q)

Calibrated to today's curve. Says what the market pays for. The Q long-run mean sits above the P mean when investors demand a term premium for holding duration.

Expectations-only yield

The average of the expected short rate under P over the bond's life. Observed yield minus this is a crude, model-based term premium. Compare with the affine estimate on the Term Premium tab.
